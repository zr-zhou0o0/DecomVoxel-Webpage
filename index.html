Intro / Guided 3D-native priors

Complete the unseen. Preserve the scene.

We propose DecomVoxel, a framework that integrates guided 3D-native priors to enhance decompositional scene reconstruction. By formulating object completion as guided in-situ optimization, our method achieves high-quality topology, geometry, and appearance for both individual objects and backgrounds while strictly preserving original spatial layouts.

Method / Guided in-situ reconstruction

Method Overview

Our framework bridges 3D generative priors with neural reconstruction through a guided in-situ denoising optimization. We segment objects from the initial scene and then sequentially optimize their geometry and appearance under adaptive spatial guidance. This process recovers missing structures and consistent textures while preserving the original scene layout, producing high-fidelity topology, geometry, and appearance.

01 / Preliminaries

In-situ Denoising Optimization

We encode each incomplete object segment into a latent representation and optimize it directly in the original scene coordinate system. Instead of uniformly trusting flow-matching gradients at every noise level, we reformulate the objective around the predicted noise residual.

  • Scene-aligned refinement. Completion occurs in situ, avoiding a separate generation-and-alignment stage that can introduce pose, scale, and rotation errors.
  • Epsilon-based distillation. The predicted residual ε̂θ = xt + (1 − t)vθ(xt, t) supervises the latent against sampled noise.
  • Adaptive gradient strength. The factor (1 − t) suppresses unreliable high-noise updates and strengthens precise low-noise refinement.

02 / Anchor

Adaptive Spatial Guidance

Spatial evidence from the reconstructed scene constrains the stochastic 3D prior. Two complementary anchor sets preserve verified content and prevent geometry from growing into prohibited regions.

  • Occupied anchor ΩO. Low-uncertainty voxels retain their initial latent features through a structural preservation loss.
  • Vacant anchor ΩV. Ray-cast free space and regions occupied by other instances identify where the current object must not exist.
  • Redistribution and annealing. Vacant features are sampled near a learned void prior rather than hard-zeroed, while noise level and anchor weights decay over time to balance synthesis and fidelity.

03 / Optimize

Optimization

The complete scene is recovered in three sequential stages so that background completion, object geometry, and texture refinement can use the most suitable priors while remaining in one coordinate frame.

  • Decompositional reconstruction. GeoSVR produces a global sparse voxel scene, instance masks partition objects and background, and geometric uncertainty initializes reliable anchors.
  • Background completion. Unobserved colors are inpainted and planar depth constraints support re-optimization before extracting the background mesh.
  • Object refinement. A structural grid first recovers complete geometry; DINOv2 features back-projected from multiple views then initialize appearance latents for texture synthesis.
